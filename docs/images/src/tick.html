What one tick does

Every minute, in this order. A lock keeps two ticks from overlapping. No agent ever runs.

1
Read the workers' reportsDone with a PR → ready-for-human. Failed, asked or escalated → needs-human.
2
Catch dead workersA worker that exited without a report → needs-human.
3
Tear down merged issuesA PR merged on the tracker → release the worker, remove the worktree, close the issue.
4
Close finished parentsA story whose children are all closed gets closed too.
5
Fill free slotsClaim each eligible issue assigned to you, up to max_workers, and start a worker on it.Eligible: open, ready-for-agent, assigned to you (or a child of a parent you hold), and no open blocker.
