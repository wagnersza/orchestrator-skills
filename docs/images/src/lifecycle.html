The life of an issue

Four state labels. Only the script moves them, except that you write ready-for-agent.

ready-for-agent
You label it and assign it to yourself.
tick claims it,
or "work on N"
──▶
in-progress
A worker builds it in its own worktree.
worker reports done
and a PR is open
──▶
ready-for-human
You review the PR.
"merge N", or you
merge on the tracker
──▶
closed
Worker released, worktree removed, issue closed.
When a worker gets stuck It fails, asks a question, or dies: the tick moves the issue to needs-human and posts the worker's own words as a comment. Nothing re-prompts it.

You answer on the issue and relabel it ready-for-agent. A fresh worker resumes in the same worktree and reads your answer first.
